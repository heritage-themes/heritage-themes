# Heritage Themes for Pulsar Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed

- The terminal colours keep WCAG AA contrast on the selection, in focused and unfocused views. In 40 themes the red, green, yellow, blue, magenta or cyan that fell short is a shade lighter in dark themes or darker in light ones, with its hue kept.

## [1.24.0] - 2026-11-24

### Fixed

- Code and text keep WCAG AA contrast on the theme's green and red highlight backgrounds, as on notifications and input validation. In 44 themes those backgrounds are a shade lighter in light themes or darker in dark ones, with their hue and depth of colour kept.

## [1.21.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added

- A UI theme and a syntax theme for each Heritage theme, which colour the workspace and the editor in Pulsar 1.113
  or later.
