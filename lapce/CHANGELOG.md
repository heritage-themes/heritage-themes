# Heritage Themes for Lapce Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed

- The terminal colours keep WCAG AA contrast on the selection, in focused and unfocused views. In 40 themes the red, green, yellow, blue, magenta or cyan that fell short is a shade lighter in dark themes or darker in light ones, with its hue kept.

## [1.24.0] - 2026-11-24

### Fixed

- Code and text keep WCAG AA contrast on the theme's green and red highlight backgrounds, as on the lines that Error Lens marks with an error. In 44 themes those backgrounds are a shade lighter in light themes or darker in dark ones, with their hue and depth of colour kept.

### Added
