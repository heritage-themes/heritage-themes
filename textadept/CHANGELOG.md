## [Unreleased]

## [1.11.1] - 2026-11-14

### Fixed

- Preprocessor directives, such as C and C++ `#include` and `#define`, no longer take the keyword colour. They take a colour of their own, which also stands apart from comments.

## [1.7.0] - 2026-11-05

### Added

- Themes for Textadept, one for each Heritage theme. They colour code, the line numbers and fold margin, the caret and caret line, selections, brace matches, call tips, markers, indicators and ANSI output from the theme's palette.
