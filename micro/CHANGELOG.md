# Heritage Themes for Micro Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [1.25.0] - 2026-11-25

### Fixed

- The terminal colours keep WCAG AA contrast on the selection, in focused and unfocused views. In 40 themes the red, green, yellow, blue, magenta or cyan that fell short is a shade lighter in dark themes or darker in light ones, with its hue kept.

## [1.11.1] - 2026-11-14

### Fixed

- Preprocessor directives, such as C and C++ `#include` and `#define`, no longer take the keyword colour. They take a colour of their own, which also stands apart from comments. The same colour marks the other constructs that Micro's syntax files highlight as `preproc`, such as HTML's `<script>` and `<style>` tags.

## [1.7.0] - 2026-11-05

### Fixed

- Code keeps WCAG AA contrast on the current line's highlight. In 33 themes, the code colours that fell just below 4.5:1 on it are a shade darker in light themes or lighter in dark ones.
- The current line's number in Kerala Kasavu (Light) reaches 4.5:1.

## [1.6.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added

- Colour schemes for Micro, one for each Heritage theme. They colour the editor, selections, line numbers, the cursor line, search matches, the gutter's marks, the status line, the tab bar, split dividers and the completion menu from the theme's palette.
