# Heritage Themes Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Wuxing Day and Wuxing Night, drawn from Chinese colour symbolism and the Five Elements.
- Danqing Ink and Danqing Paper, drawn from Chinese painting pigments.
- Shuimo Night and Shuimo Day, drawn from Chinese ink-wash painting.
- Holi Night and Holi Day, drawn from Holi, the Indian festival of colours.
- Kalamkari Indigo and Kalamkari Cotton, drawn from the natural dyes of Kalamkari hand-painted cloth.
- Mughal Night and Mughal Wasli, drawn from the pigments of Mughal miniature painting.
- Rajasthan Blue City and Rajasthan Pink City, drawn from the cities and cloth of Rajasthan.
- Kerala Backwaters and Kerala Kasavu, drawn from Kerala's backwaters, Kathakali and the kasavu loom.
- Bengal Baluchari and Bengal Laal Paar, drawn from the looms of Bengal and Durga Puja.
- Tamil Kolam and Tamil Olai, drawn from the kolam, palm-leaf manuscripts and Kanchipuram silk of Tamil Nadu.
- Punjab Phulkari and Punjab Basant, drawn from phulkari embroidery and the mustard fields of Basant.
- Gujarat Navratri and Gujarat Rann, drawn from Navratri, the Rann of Kutch and patola silk.
- Morocco Majorelle, Morocco Marrakech and Morocco Zellige, drawn from the walls of Morocco.
- Peru Kulli and Peru Puka, drawn from the natural dyes of Andean weaving in Peru.
- Kashubia Zemia and Kashubia Niebò, drawn from the seven colours of Kashubian embroidery.
- Złotnica Aksamit and Złotnica Płótno, drawn from the gold-and-silver embroidered bonnets of Kashubia.
- Łowicz Wycinanka and Łowicz Kodra, drawn from the striped wool and paper cut-outs of Łowicz.
- Zalipie Sadza and Zalipie Wapno, drawn from the painted cottages of Zalipie.
- Podhale Kłobuk and Podhale Sukno, drawn from the Highlander costume of Podhale.
- Sardinia Orbace and Sardinia Camisa, drawn from the dyed wool, linen and silk of Sardinian traditional dress.
- Hmong Batik and Hmong Hemp, drawn from the indigo batik and cross-stitch of the Hmong pleated skirt.
- Ndebele Nzima and Ndebele Mhlophe, drawn from the outlined wall painting of the Ndebele homestead.
- Wixárika Haramara and Wixárika Wirikuta, drawn from the yarn paintings, beadwork and five colours of maize of the Wixárika.
- Maya Corte and Maya Huipil, drawn from the town dress of the Guatemalan Maya and the K'iche' colours of the four roads.
- Yolŋu Galawu and Yolŋu Gapaṉ, drawn from the ochres of Yolŋu bark painting in north-east Arnhem Land.
- Ainu Kaparamip and Ainu Attus, drawn from the appliqué robes and four colour words of the Ainu.
- Shaker Prussian and Shaker Plaster, drawn from the paint of the Shaker villages and the Millennial Laws.
- Kalaallisuut Qernertoq and Kalaallisuut Qaqortoq, drawn from the colours for age and marital status in the West Greenlandic national dress.
- Mudhif Qir and Mudhif Qasab, drawn from the reed guest houses of the Marsh Arabs of southern Iraq.
- Maramureș Zadie and Maramureș Cânepă, drawn from the striped aprons and flowered skirts of Maramureș, coloured by age.
- Aizome Kachi and Aizome Kamenozoki, drawn from the forty-eight shades of Japanese indigo dyeing.
