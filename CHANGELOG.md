# Heritage Themes Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- Islands UI for every theme: each palette has a main window colour, and the editor and tool windows sit on it as islands. Needs a 2025.3 or later IDE.
- Theme names that do not say Night or Day end in (Dark) or (Light), so the IDE theme list shows each theme's brightness.

- Wuxing Day and Wuxing Night, drawn from Chinese colour symbolism and the Five Elements.
- Danqing Ink (Dark) and Danqing Paper (Light), drawn from Chinese painting pigments.
- Shuimo Night and Shuimo Day, drawn from Chinese ink-wash painting.
- Holi Night and Holi Day, drawn from Holi, the Indian festival of colours.
- Kalamkari Indigo (Dark) and Kalamkari Cotton (Light), drawn from the natural dyes of Kalamkari hand-painted cloth.
- Mughal Night and Mughal Wasli (Light), drawn from the pigments of Mughal miniature painting.
- Rajasthan Blue City (Dark) and Rajasthan Pink City (Light), drawn from the cities and cloth of Rajasthan.
- Kerala Backwaters (Dark) and Kerala Kasavu (Light), drawn from Kerala's backwaters, Kathakali and the kasavu loom.
- Bengal Baluchari (Dark) and Bengal Laal Paar (Light), drawn from the looms of Bengal and Durga Puja.
- Tamil Kolam (Dark) and Tamil Olai (Light), drawn from the kolam, palm-leaf manuscripts and Kanchipuram silk of Tamil Nadu.
- Punjab Phulkari (Dark) and Punjab Basant (Light), drawn from phulkari embroidery and the mustard fields of Basant.
- Gujarat Navratri (Dark) and Gujarat Rann (Light), drawn from Navratri, the Rann of Kutch and patola silk.
- Morocco Majorelle (Dark), Morocco Marrakech (Dark) and Morocco Zellige (Dark), drawn from the walls of Morocco.
- Peru Kulli (Dark) and Peru Puka (Dark), drawn from the natural dyes of Andean weaving in Peru.
- Kashubia Zemia (Dark) and Kashubia Niebò (Light), drawn from the seven colours of Kashubian embroidery.
- Złotnica Aksamit (Dark) and Złotnica Płótno (Light), drawn from the gold-and-silver embroidered bonnets of Kashubia.
- Łowicz Wycinanka (Dark) and Łowicz Kodra (Light), drawn from the striped wool and paper cut-outs of Łowicz.
- Zalipie Sadza (Dark) and Zalipie Wapno (Light), drawn from the painted cottages of Zalipie.
- Podhale Kłobuk (Dark) and Podhale Sukno (Light), drawn from the Highlander costume of Podhale.
- Sardinia Orbace (Dark) and Sardinia Camisa (Light), drawn from the dyed wool, linen and silk of Sardinian traditional dress.
- Hmong Batik (Dark) and Hmong Hemp (Light), drawn from the indigo batik and cross-stitch of the Hmong pleated skirt.
- Ndebele Nzima (Dark) and Ndebele Mhlophe (Light), drawn from the outlined wall painting of the Ndebele homestead.
- Wixárika Haramara (Dark) and Wixárika Wirikuta (Light), drawn from the yarn paintings, beadwork and five colours of maize of the Wixárika.
- Maya Corte (Dark) and Maya Huipil (Light), drawn from the town dress of the Guatemalan Maya and the K'iche' colours of the four roads.
- Yolŋu Galawu (Dark) and Yolŋu Gapaṉ (Light), drawn from the ochres of Yolŋu bark painting in north-east Arnhem Land.
- Ainu Kaparamip (Dark) and Ainu Attus (Light), drawn from the appliqué robes and four colour words of the Ainu.
- Shaker Prussian (Dark) and Shaker Plaster (Light), drawn from the paint of the Shaker villages and the Millennial Laws.
- Kalaallisuut Qernertoq (Dark) and Kalaallisuut Qaqortoq (Light), drawn from the colours for age and marital status in the West Greenlandic national dress.
- Mudhif Qir (Dark) and Mudhif Qasab (Light), drawn from the reed guest houses of the Marsh Arabs of southern Iraq.
- Maramureș Zadie (Dark) and Maramureș Cânepă (Light), drawn from the striped aprons and flowered skirts of Maramureș, coloured by age.
- Aizome Kachi (Dark) and Aizome Kamenozoki (Light), drawn from the forty-eight shades of Japanese indigo dyeing.
- Edo Cha (Dark) and Edo Nezumi (Light), drawn from the forty-eight browns and hundred greys of Edo-period dress.
