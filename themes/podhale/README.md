# Podhale

Dark and light themes based on the costume of the Highlanders (*górale*) of Podhale, below the Tatra mountains in southern Poland: crimson, black and off-white, with the colours of the *parzenica*. Provides two UI themes, **Podhale Kłobuk** and **Podhale Sukno**, each with a matching editor colour scheme.

![Preview](preview.png)

## Inspiration

The *parzenica* on the white woollen trousers (*portki*) began as a loop of cord reinforcing the slit, first in red, then in red and navy. Green, pink and blue were added as it grew into embroidery.

| Colour | Source | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Czerwień (crimson) | The first cord of the parzenica; the band of the hat | Keywords, caret, buttons and the Run widget, Spock labels |
| Granat (navy) | The second cord of the parzenica | Strings |
| Mosiądz (brass) | Buckles of the shepherd's belt (*pas bacowski*) and the shirt clasp (*spinka*) | Methods |
| Zieleń (green) | The embroidery of the parzenica and the *cucha* | Fields |
| Niebieski (blue) | The embroidery of the parzenica | Types |
| Korale (coral) | Red coral necklaces | Numbers, constants |
| Róż (pink) | The embroidery of the cucha | Annotations |

Podhale Kłobuk (*kłobuk*, hat) has the black felt hat, with its red band and white shells, as its ground. Podhale Sukno (*sukno*, broadcloth) is the white woollen cloth of the trousers and the cucha. Hex values are interpretations of the cloth, threads and metal, adjusted for contrast against each background.

## Develop

The palettes are in `theme.py`. The colour mappings and the preview are shared by every theme family and live in [`heritage/`](../../heritage/). See the [root README](../../README.md) for build, preview and install steps.
