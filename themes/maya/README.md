# Maya

Dark and light themes based on the backstrap-woven dress of the Maya towns of highland Guatemala, read through the K'iche' colours of the four roads and the centre. Provides two UI themes, **Maya Corte (Dark)** and **Maya Huipil (Light)**, each with a matching editor colour scheme.

![Preview](preview.png)

## Inspiration

In the Guatemalan highlands, a woman's *traje* identifies her town: each town has its own huipil (blouse) and corte (wrap skirt). Huipils are brocaded in red, yellow, green and purple, often on white cotton; Santiago Atitlán and San Juan Sacatepéquez are known for purple and violet. Cortes are *jaspe*, cotton tie-dyed in *añil* (indigo). In K'iche', four colours name the four roads of the world and one names its centre: *kaq* (red, the east), *saq* (white, the north), *q'eq* (black, the west), *q'än* (yellow, the south) and *räx*, the green-blue of the centre and the road of life. *Räx* covers both green and blue: green like a tree, blue like the sky.

| Colour | Name | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green, the centre | *räx* | Keywords, Spock labels |
| Red, the east | *kaq* | Strings; buttons and the Run widget |
| Yellow, the south | *q'än* | Types |
| Purple | *morado* | Method declarations and calls |
| Indigo | *añil* | Fields |
| Sky blue | *räx* | Numbers, constants, annotations, links |
| White, the north | *saq* | The ground of Maya Huipil (Light) |

Maya Corte (Dark) has the indigo jaspe of the corte as its ground; Maya Huipil (Light) has the white cotton of the huipil. Colours vary by town and by weaver; hex values are interpretations of the dyed threads, adjusted for contrast against each background.
